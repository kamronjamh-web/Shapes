Run inventory and ground equipment

Five active shapes and two reserve shapes are separate collections. Combat cycling,
support Wings, and active effects use only the active collection. Tab shows both;
previewing a reserve is read-only, and Swap with held shape exchanges exact items
including their installed parts. Wing compatibility is validated on active insertion
and swaps. Player health percentage is preserved when the held shape changes.

Loose modulator storage holds 100 parts. Installed parts do not consume this limit.
Picking up, buying for storage, and returning installed parts enforce the limit.
Inventory drop buttons place the original item on the ground and free its slot.
The final active shape cannot be dropped.

Weapons and modulators require aiming at their ground model and pressing F.
Interaction uses short range and an unobstructed world ray. A focused item takes
priority over the nearby shop shortcut. Full storage leaves equipment untouched.
Coins keep automatic proximity pickup and wave-clear sweep behavior.
Wave rewards are placed near the player, including the final reward; after victory,
Collect ground loot restores field movement and interaction while combat stays over.
Escape returns to the results menu.

Ground weapons render their base geometric shapes inside animated energy pillars;
the assembled weapon model is used after equipping the shape in the MultiWeapon.
Ground modulator cartridges have a 0.26 m longest axis, half their previous 0.52 m
size. Installed cartridges and inventory thumbnails retain their original scale.
All cartridge views share canonical rarity accent colors matching the pillar.
PowerTier 1/2/3 is shown by exactly 1/2/3 accent notches; quality does not alter these
notches. Authored excess notches are hidden and missing notches are added per instance.

Enemy weapon rewards launch upward and outward with randomized direction and spin,
using cosmetic randomness separate from reward rolls. Temporary rigid bodies collide
with the world, excluding player/enemy/other-loot collisions. They settle on sleep or
after 2.5 seconds into static collectible items. At most 64 flight bodies are active;
overflow settles the oldest body and retains every item. Pause freezes flight without
consuming lifetime. Successful pickup removes the body; full inventory leaves it and
the exact item intact. Arena rebuild settles active bodies before regrounding loot.
Pillars track the ground beneath flight, while aiming targets the airborne shape.
Sparks begin after settling, avoiding repeated emitter resets during motion. Settlement
and regrounding exclude characters and retain supported roofs/platforms. Cartridge
accent materials remain owned per instance so inspection recoloring cannot bleed.

The supplied LootPillarAtlas.png preserves the original 2172x724 artwork: six 362x724
frames, also exposed as individual AtlasTexture resources in Textures/Loot/Frames.
The GPU blends adjacent frames at eight frames per second, including the last-to-first
transition. Each item has a stable phase offset. Two transparent planes cross at
right angles, with the pair oriented toward the camera for consistent volume.
An upward-scrolling energy pattern overlays the atlas; a bright white center fades
into soft rarity-colored edges. A separate horizontal glow anchors the item at
ground level. All three planes share the same instanced mesh and draw batch.
Weapon rarity and modulator PowerTier (not quality) determine color, height and width.
The black background contributes no light. Pillars use additive blending, world depth
testing, mipmapped sampling, and no shadows, lights, collision or CPU animation updates.
Actual GPU particles add occasional rising sparks. A pool selects the nearest
32 equipment items within 45 m every 0.25 s. Weapon ranks 0-5 and modulator PowerTier
ranks 0/2/4 control particle count, size and speed; item quality does not affect them.
Each active emitter holds 3-13 particles. Collected items immediately hide/stop their
emitter; reassignment clears old particles, while retained items keep their animation.
Distant beams and ground glows remain visible without distant particle emitters.
The aimed card shows rarity, quality where applicable, level, sockets, own-item base
stats and summed bonuses. Effective damage/health already include item bonuses;
bonus rows explain those totals and should not be applied a second time.

Equipment data survives wave clear and arena live rebuild. Models live outside the
arena geometry root and are grounded again after rebuild. Equipment is never collected
or discarded by the rendering budget: MaxLoosePickups now also bounds nearby visual
instances (8–256, default 128), with equipment taking priority over coin visuals.
Only models within 120 m materialize, up to eight new visuals per refresh. Equipment
outside that budget retains exact data and its pillar remains rendered. Pillars use
independent 128 m spatial MultiMesh batches with reusable buffers, refreshed on item
addition, collection or regrounding. Normal camera frustum and world occlusion still
apply; the model distance and count budget do not hide equipment pillars. Currency
keeps its older overflow collection policy. No 50k horde FPS claim is made by this change.
