The director exposes ExplosionAudioSettings for an optional catalog override.

Size comes from the final gameplay damage radius, after weapon/modulator scaling:
  Small: radius below 3 m
  Medium: radius at least 3 m and below 8 m
  Large: radius at least 8 m
MediumRadiusStart and LargeRadiusStart are editable catalog settings.
These are initial tuning values, not a user-specified fixed balance rule.

All six slots exist: small/medium/large, each close/far. Only the supplied medium
recording is available. Both medium slots reference the same imported WAV.
An absent distance partner uses its size partner first; absent small/large pairs
temporarily use medium. Replace those slots when their recordings are supplied.

Same-source close/far plays one AudioStreamPlayer3D, with continuous native distance
attenuation and high-frequency filtering. No second asset, doubled playback or
global filter bus is needed. Current tuning: close UnitSize 24 m, max distance
160 m, authored gain -12 dB, distant attenuation filter 2500 Hz/-24 dB.
The close band (up to 24 m) has zero filter attenuation, preserving the original
attack. Shelf strength ramps with a smoothstep of physical distance to 100 m.
This corrects the previous constant -24 dB filter setting: Godot factors authored
volume into shelf strength, so that setting also muffled close explosions.
Each accepted detonation varies pitch uniformly within +/-1.5 semitones (roughly
0.917-1.091 playback speed), configurable with PitchVariationSemitones. Close/far
partners share one sampled pitch; listener movement never retunes a playing voice.
The audio-only random generator is separate from combat and other weapon sounds.
If distinct close/far recordings are assigned later, paired voices crossfade with
equal-power gains between 24 and 100 m. FarDistance controls the crossfade endpoint
and full distance-filter strength for both single-source and distinct-clip playback.

Four reusable voice pairs and a 90 ms global event interval bound explosion audio.
The supplied identical medium pair uses at most four simultaneous voices. Dense
events are coalesced and oldest voices reused; not every chain event starts a sound.
Damage and visual timing are unaffected. Playback respects pause, sound enable,
the existing SFX bus/slider, fallback Master routing, and run clear/restart.

Actual detonation paths: nova, manual wrath burst, mines, mortars, command-cluster
detonations, collapse pulse, delayed cluster bursts, event-horizon expiry, reactor
burst and player-weapon Volitile. Each passes its actual damage radius. Victim hit
sounds are suppressed within the explosion damage scope, restored in finally.
Continuous fields, passive aura ticks, lightning and clone bullet hits retain their
existing audio rather than repeatedly playing an explosion. Existing secondary-proc
guards, source identity, critical handling and damage queries are preserved.
