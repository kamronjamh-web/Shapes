Modulator quality and rarity - 2026-10-09

Quality controls the strength of a part's benefit:
  Broken <name>       50%
  <name>             100% (Baseline, no name prefix)
  Enhanced <name>     150%
  Flawless <name>     200%
Tier 1/2/3 describes effect rarity. Every tier can roll every quality.
Rarity colors are gray/blue/purple; the name and power percentage show quality.
The shape's own rarity still determines the gun's accent color.

Ordinary rolls, shops, and completed defense-wave rewards use one loot service.
Quality odds: 60% Broken, 30% Baseline, 9% Enhanced, 1% Flawless.
Effect rarity odds: 70% Tier 1, 25% Tier 2, 5% Tier 3.
Rarity is selected before the effect's within-tier weight and independent quality
roll. Restricted shop pools renormalize among available rarity tiers. Future skill
bonuses can pass a modified immutable ModulatorLootProfile without changing saved
items or catalog templates. No skill-tree bonus is active yet. Existing Luck still
changes drop frequency and coin yields, not these quality weights.

Each normal completed FPS defense wave grants exactly one loose modulator using
the same roll, including the final wave. It is separate from enemy drops (the base
chances remain 0.5% shape and 1% modulator). Duplicate wave-clear callbacks cannot
grant another reward. Manual horde testing does not grant completion rewards.
The victory screen's Final reward / workshop button opens the completed run's
shop, with combat stopped. Run loot/fusion remains local to that FPS run; it does
not automatically export to the persistent hub inventory.

Fusion is available in the FPS field shop:
  two identical loose parts of the same quality + coins -> one next quality
Rarity tier and raw effect payload stay the same. Installed parts can be returned
to storage, and shop parts can be bought into storage even with a full weapon.
No fusion beyond Flawless; retired Apex parts cannot be fused or newly installed.
Default cost = 20 coins x rarity tier (1..3) x current quality step (1..3).
                 Broken -> Base   Base -> Enhanced   Enhanced -> Flawless
  Tier 1               20                40                    60
  Tier 2               40                80                   120
  Tier 3               60               120                   180
The grave menu's Motion tab can adjust the base cost for tuning. Missing copies,
insufficient coins, a closed shop, and stale callbacks leave both inputs and coins
unchanged. Saved custom parts must also match raw payloads to fuse, preventing a
custom/old part from being silently replaced with today's catalog definition.

Scaling rules:
- Strength applies to the modulator contribution, not total weapon damage.
  A baseline +12% damage bonus becomes +6/+12/+18/+24% across the four qualities.
- Multiplier bonuses scale around their neutral value of 1. Attack speed scales
  the rate benefit, then converts back to cooldown. Fixed penalties remain fixed.
- Defining behavior survives at Broken. Extra output, pierce, bounces, ricochets,
  and chain counts retain fractional benefits; FPS combat carries the fraction
  per source/purpose across eligible attacks instead of rounding it away.
- Transformation/proc damage or its defining potency scales. Trigger thresholds,
  recovery times, secondary lifetimes, and fixed burst/fragment topology stay
  fixed, avoiding quality multiplying several dimensions of the same effect.
- Tight Orbit's intended radius reduction scales; Orbital Compression's tighter
  radius remains its fixed tradeoff. Wing cadence is 25/50/75/100% of host rate.
- Volitile's radius is sum(copy strengths) + strongest strength, capped at 4 m;
  its 50% killing-hit damage ratio remains fixed. A single copy is 1/2/3/4 m.
- Nonstacking transforms, Critical Cascade and Omni Feedback use the strongest
  quality installed copy. Installation order cannot replace Flawless with Broken.
- Existing attack caps and copied/sustained-hit eligibility restrictions remain.

Item descriptions and comparisons use the resolved combat payload. Comparisons
show per-part values against the strongest installed copy of the same effect;
they are not a promise of total weapon DPS after caps and other interactions.

Apex Doctrine is retired: no new drops, shop offers, installation, or active
amplification. Saved copies and key lookup are retained for a later replacement.
There are 99 active catalog effects plus the preserved Apex definition.

Save compatibility: the old numeric Flawed enum value and 'flawed' parser alias
remain valid, but visible naming is Broken. Saved baseline effect/substat payloads
and quality are retained. Effective values are resolved into cached detached data;
saving, cloning, loading and fusion never write resolved potency back as baseline.
Known-key saves with a raw payload retain it; old key-only saves use the catalog.

Validation entry points:
  Tools/Validation/RunModulatorQualityValidation.ps1
  Tools/Validation/RunFpsModulatorValidation.ps1
  Tools/Validation/RunWeaponInspectionValidation.ps1
  Tools/Validation/RunWeaponInspectionCapture.ps1 -Modulators -QualityWorkshop
All accept -GodotPath and isolate test saves below .tmp. The capture runner also
accepts -Shapes 'square,pyramid,klein,boys' for a focused rendered review.
Legacy arena count consumers receive fractional carry and resolved potency;
dormant legacy patterns that already ignored output/ricochet/bounce are outside
this FPS pass. Automated contracts do not establish whole-catalog balance or
sustained 2,000-enemy combat performance.
