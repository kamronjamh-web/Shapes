STEPPED STRONGHOLD SQUARE TURRETS

The stronghold field shop manages four fixed balcony mounts. Every shop restock
includes a Square shape, so the first defense does not depend on a shape drop.
Defaults: unlock 20 coins, repair 10 coins, 250 mount HP, 100 m base range,
0.35 s acquisition interval. These controls, muzzle height and model scale are
exported on FpsVerticalSlice. Upgrade uses the existing shape upgrade price.

Buy a spare Square, install compatible parts in Tab, then use the shop's mount
button. The exact run-owned shape and its installed part references leave player
storage and enter the mount; no item is cloned. At least one player shape remains
equipped. A return needs free shape storage and preserves all installed parts.
To edit mounted parts, return the shape first. A disabled turret keeps its shape
and can be repaired through the shop. Live fort rebuilds preserve equipment and
wounded mount health. Paused/victory presentation updates without advancing fire
or damage. Disabling the stepped layout hides turrets and clears their bullets,
while retaining mounted equipment for reenabling the layout.

The prototype deliberately accepts only Square and installed passive damage,
cooldown, area, range, projectile speed, critical chance/damage, projectile count
and piercing effects. Unsupported installed effects, including Wings, transforms,
on-fire/on-hit procs, healing and player movement effects, block mounting with a
specific reason. Player-only base substats do not affect a mount. Shapes with a
player-only intrinsic substat are still usable as Square turrets.

Each mount has independent HP, cooldown, fractional output/pierce carry and target
state. Bullets are independent from the player weapon effect lists, carry their
source item, and apply directional damage without player lifesteal or player
proc/Wing scheduling. The prototype caps eight bullets per volley, nine piercing
hits per bullet and 96 live turret bullets across all mounts. Physical world
obstacles and real enemy capsules stop/intercept those bullets.

Targets stay in the mount's cardinal sector. Priorities select nearby or toughest
visible candidates. Acquisition rotates through local spatial cells with a limit
of 2,048 cells, 512 visited records, 128 returned candidates and twelve candidate
visibility rays per refresh. Dense cells use a bounded sample; this does not
guarantee the globally nearest/strongest enemy. Contact damage separately samples
up to 128 records/32 candidates within 4 m and checks actual head distance, dealing
8 HP/second per qualifying enemy. This is a proximity defense prototype, not yet
a collider/objective integrated into the horde's melee/projectile target solver.

RunFortTurretValidation.ps1 validates the actual shop purchase/unlock, exact item
ownership, supported/unsupported installation policy, independent moving bullets,
player effect reset isolation, layout toggles, rebuild survival, disable/repair,
capacity-blocked returns and paused presentation. Current results: 28 headless
checks, 30 native checks including mounted-model and scrolled-shop screenshots.
This is functional evidence with a controlled target, not a large-horde combat
benchmark or exhaustive balance validation of every modulator combination.
