Modulator impact pass - 2026-10-09

Direction confirmed by the user
Replace overlapping stat-only parts with weapon-changing effects. Keep a small,
clear set of useful stat upgrades. The proposals below are prepared for the next
implementation pass; these replacement effects have not shipped. The separate
quality/rarity implementation is documented in ModulatorQuality.txt.

Foundation completed in this pass
- One WeaponModulatorCompatibility check serves hub inventory, run assembly,
  FPS/shop/debug installation and inspection. Existing FPS eligibility is retained.
- Mobius now produces N initial shots and N base delayed shots, preserving each
  shot's direction, source, delay and damage. Generic modulator echoes still replay
  their full weapon pattern.
- Inspection explains installed effects, selected-only player stats/reactors,
  Wing activity, Apex retirement, Mimic limitations, initial hits versus sustained
  ticks, and the shape-specific meaning of extra output/duration. Equipping
  refreshes those explanations even when the preview selection does not change.

Existing behaviors deliberately left for a balance decision
1. Primary Mimic's copied damage cannot roll crits or trigger hit effects. Critical
   Cascade, Omni Feedback and crit parts can consequently become ineffective on
   that damage. Mobius/Boy's Surface copied patterns fall back to Square.
   Recommendation: separate permission to crit from permission to trigger another
   effect before reconsidering Mimic. Preserve bounded secondary generations.
2. Triangle/Cone sustained ticks cannot roll crits or apply hit effects. Existing
   marks, damage multipliers and lifesteal can still benefit those ticks.
   Recommendation: keep the current rule for this catalog pass, then evaluate
   normalized proc frequency per second instead of letting every tick chain.
3. Klein attack-speed and output bonuses affect manual bursts, not its aura.
   Klein's aura/reactor and player health/movement/regen/dodge/luck bonuses require
   the weapon to be selected. Recommendation: retain and explain that distinction;
   use an explicit aura-cadence effect if we later want faster aura ticks.
4. Apex Doctrine has been retired at the user's request because tiers now denote
   rarity. It cannot roll or be newly installed and grants no amplification.
   Owned copies remain preserved for a later replacement; no replacement is active.

Small combat-stat set to retain
Sharpened Form (damage), Quickened Core (attack cadence), Expanded Shape (area),
Long Reach (reach), Accelerated Flight (travel speed), Lasting Pattern (duration).
These are a proposed core, not a decision to remove all other stat parts at once.
Keep defensive, movement and crit choices for a later focused consolidation.
Power Coil / Siege Core / Reinforced Geometry and Accelerator Fins remain later
overlap candidates; changing seven parts is enough for the first comparison.

Recommended first prototypes

P1 - Ballistic Tuning -> Impulse Rail
Current: projectile speed x1.15, weaker overlap with Accelerated Flight x1.20.
Weapon: Square first; evaluate Hypercone after the interaction is established.
Proposed: every third direct shot that hits an enemy pushes a short rectangular
lane through the crowd behind that impact. Show the charged shot on the weapon,
then a visible line and heavier impact sound when it releases.
Payoff: creates breathing room at a piled gate. Cost: lower ordinary-shot damage
and dependence on lining up the crowd. Not an additional explosion/echo mod.
Prototype limits: one impulse per 0.5 s per weapon, up to eight displaced enemies;
query the existing spatial grid. Secondary attacks cannot charge or release it.
Confirm displacement works with crowd contact, piling, walls and elites.

P1 - Beam Condenser -> Sweep Conductor
Current: damage x1.22 / area x0.82; overlaps Line Driver x1.35 / area x0.75.
Weapon: Triangle.
Proposed: the sustained beam follows the player's aim through its existing short
life, letting the player actively sweep a cutting line across the horde.
Payoff: directional control and visible movement. Cost: shorter reach and weaker
initial hit. Preserve current sustained-tick crit/proc rules for the prototype.
Prototype limits: reuse the existing beam record and one segment query per tick;
only the original beam follows aim. Prism Refraction's delayed branches retain
their independent directions. Wing/mimic beams retain their source direction.
Check aiming, switching weapons, pause, camera occlusion and source ownership.

P1 - Fuse Tuner -> Crowd Fuse
Current: cooldown x0.92; overlaps Quickened Core and other mine cadence packages.
Weapon: Hexagon.
Proposed: an armed mine waits for three enemies in its footprint, then releases
a stronger, wider crowd blast. Three occupancy lights show the trigger condition.
Payoff: rewards placement at a choke point. Cost: isolated enemies may walk past
without detonating it. Exact damage/area bonuses need a matched prototype test.
Prototype limits: count at most three candidates at 10 Hz per mine; preserve mine
lifetime and existing active-mine limits. Command Cluster's manual detonation
overrides waiting but does not award the full crowd bonus without enough targets.
Check first-person readability from the wall, overlapping mines and target exits.

Second group, after reviewing the first three in play

P2 - Orbital Bracing -> Front Brace (Cube)
Current: range x1.12, overlaps Long Reach.
Move the existing orbitals into a controllable square ahead of the player to push
contact damage into a gate lane. Trade rear coverage and some lifetime for forward
control. This is damage positioning, not new projectile blocking. Reuse orbital
records/contact cooldowns. Define the formation's phase motion with Aegis Carousel
before allowing that combination; do not silently disable its transformation.

P2 - Guidance Spine -> Fan Hunt (Pyramid)
Current: homing x1.20, identical to Guiding Bias.
Seekers from a volley reserve different nearby targets, visibly spreading across
the crowd. Trade elite focus for coverage. One bounded query and at most eight
reservations per volley. Pack Hunters concentrates targets, so choose explicit
mutual exclusion or a documented precedence rule before shipping the combination.

P2 - Extended Uptime -> Recast Anchor (Tesseract, Impossible Cube)
Current: duration x1.20, overlaps Lasting Pattern x1.22.
Recast moves the existing field to the aim point, visibly folding and reforming
its frame. Trade field stacking and some area for precise repositioning. Use one
field per source and a relocation cooldown. Moving must not trigger Event Horizon
Tear; actual expiry still does. Preserve Phase Inversion's lifetime/phase state.
This narrows existing compatibility; old mine/orbital/clone installations need an
explicit migration path rather than being transformed silently.

P2 - Mortar Barrage -> Airburst Crown (Hyperphere)
Current: extra shell / lower damage; overlaps the extra-output family.
The shell opens above the landing point into a descending shock front instead of
adding another identical shell. Trade concentrated damage for crowd coverage.
Use one bounded impact query and existing pulse geometry, with damage volumes
matching the visible front. Scorch Crater occurs once at the ground target.

Save/load requirement before any replacement ships
InventoryManager now retains saved raw effect/substat payloads and quality for
known keys; only old key-only saves fall back to the catalog definition. This
prevents quality resolution from rebasing owned parts. Before a future replacement
ships, use distinct behavior keys or explicit versioned conversion, retaining old
lookup support and recovering parts that no longer fit. Existing saves must not
be silently converted to an unrelated behavior by a catalog/UI change.

Acceptance for each future prototype
- A player can see and describe the changed attack after a few shots.
- Compare against its replacement in an isolated target, a dense gate crowd, and
  a 2,000-enemy run using the same camera, population, settings and firing route.
- Confirm the payoff and tradeoff; do not label higher damage alone an improvement.
- Exercise multishot, its shape transform, Wing firing and Primary Mimic where
  installation allows them. Test unsupported combinations in the inspector too.
- Fixed caps, bounded spatial queries, one generation of secondary effects, no
  per-enemy unbounded scene spawning, and source ownership through delayed attacks.
- Cover save round trips and old installed parts before catalog/drop changes.
- Refresh the explicit 100-by-16 matrix when eligibility intentionally changes.

Validation of the foundation is recorded in LAST_CHANGES.md. Automated installation
coverage is not a claim that all 1,600 combinations were playtested for balance.
