Open SteppedStronghold.blend directly. Every wall, slab, ramp, cover and visual detail remains a named individual object.

Toggle collection 06 to inspect simplified box/wedge proxies; these are hidden from review renders.

DESIGN
200m courtyard. Four open 24m entrance lanes. Middle 122m square terrace with 12m connected circulation.
Ground elevation 8m, middle 11m, exposed 30m central fallback/core 14m.
Each primary ascent is 24m wide, rising 3m over an 18m run. Players can rotate around the middle ring.
The central platform remains reachable by the horde. The corner shop shelter sits off the main routes.
North pylons, east open apron, broken south shoulders and west low bands identify sectors without reducing route clearance.
Four 5m turret upgrade pads sit on existing outer firing balconies, one per front, with schematic shape/modulator sockets.
Flush paint reserves suggest barricade/slow-strip placement. A central repair pocket sits off the core ramp lanes.
Upgrade pieces are nonfunctional design placeholders; no specific owned item, attack behavior or economic rules are implemented.
Cyan/clay 1.8m figures are scale references, not final characters or a 50k-horde performance simulation.

PROXIES
39 axis-aligned boxes and 16 cardinal ramps, below current 64/64 GPU caps.
This study is conceptual: routing, rendering, visibility, budgets and exact collisions still need runtime integration validation.
Visual parapets, decorative pylons, rubble, the shop shelter and the objective do not claim finalized collision.
